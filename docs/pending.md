# Open verification questions

Checked on October 15, 2026. These are specific limitations of the current review, not implied acceptance or claims of absence.

## Accepted work awaiting an original manuscript

- [Retrieve the Right Signal: Decomposition-Guided Retrieval-Augmented Forecasting for Financial Markets](https://cikm2026.diag.uniroma1.it/program/): the official CIKM 2026 program confirms the paper and describes decomposition, retrieval-as-context and retrieved-future forecasting. A full original manuscript was not located. Keep its hybrid classification provisional and outside the fully read main list.

## Accessible publisher descriptions, incomplete full-text access

- [Retrieval-Augmented Framework with Adaptive Gating for Time Series Forecasting](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12204-025-2847-z): publisher abstract supports a provisional input/representation-augmentation assignment; full text was not accessible.
- [VARDiff: Vision-augmented retrieval-guided diffusion for stock forecasting](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0020025526000447): publisher method excerpts describe retrieved visual features conditioning denoising. Information Sciences 2026 is verified; exact first-online date and full text remain unverified in this review.
- [An improved retrieval-augmented long-term grouting power prediction method: Rejecting low-similarity retrievals](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0952197625027642): publisher method excerpts support a provisional output-fusion assignment. Its 2026 issue is verified; the DOI's 2025 component alone is not proof of a 2025 first-online date.

## Submission metadata and related versions

- [Self-adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Reinforcement Learning for Time Series Forecasting](https://openreview.net/forum?id=1jlci3sF4Q): original double-blind PDF is readable through public indexing; named authors, first posting date and ICLR 2026 acceptance were not primary-verified. Label preprint. Its training-only retrieval places it outside the inference-time split even if the bibliographic questions are resolved.
- [TimeRAG: It's Time for Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Time-Series Forecasting](https://openreview.net/forum?id=GvzL4LuycW): the accessible original says under review at ICLR 2025; that is not acceptance. It is distinct from Yang's DTW TimeRAG and Yixiong Xiao's 2026 TS-RAG. Its learned retrieval for stock movement, LLM-probability feedback, JSON context and distinctive Stock23 corpus closely overlap [FinSeer/FinSrag](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05878). No accessible author statement or identifier history establishes exact version identity. Keep as a possible related predecessor, do not assert a confirmed rename, and retain the historical candidate record, but do not count it as a confirmed independent paper yet. Its original public year and named-author list were not independently verified from readable primary metadata.

## Publication timing and scope candidates

Third-party indexing suggests a 2026 online release, but the official early-online date was not established. - [Adaptive Dominant Spectral Summary Guided Macro-Context Retrieval for Robust Multivariate Time Series Forecasting](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6753035), DSR: public SSRN record dated May 12, 2026, with a forthcoming [Expert Systems with Applications article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0957417426033713) assigned to a February 2027 issue. - [RATFM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02081): anomaly detection using forecasting; relevant to a broader applications appendix, not automatically a core forecasting-evaluation entry.

- SpecReTF has an earlier ICLR 2026 submission and a June 2026 arXiv version; acceptance was not confirmed, so it remains preprint.
- *When Does Retrieval Help Time-Series Forecasting?* exposes an arXiv submission-history date inconsistent with the month suggested by its identifier. The explicit source date is preserved with a warning, rather than silently “corrected” from the identifier.
- SteerCast's NeurIPS 2026 poster URL was verified in the official Downloads page, although its direct poster-page body was not readable through the research tool.
- SEER's public repository was accessible but implementation files were not verified; a code link is not a claim of a complete implementation.
- TIMBRE's stated ICASSP 2027 submission is not an acceptance. It remains preprint.

## How to close these gaps

Use an author-posted manuscript, an official proceedings/publisher page, an OpenReview decision/history page, or an author statement explicitly connecting versions. Do not promote a venue from a third-party list, infer dates from identifier prefixes, or merge similarly named papers without identity evidence.
