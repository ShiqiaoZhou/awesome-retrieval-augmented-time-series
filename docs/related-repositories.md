# Existing literature repositories

Checked on October 15, 2026. The public search found useful overlapping lists, but did not establish a dedicated, comprehensive catalogue with the same scope and two-view organization. This is a search result, not a claim that no such repository exists or that a new list would be the first.

## Closest explicit subcategory

### Multimodal Time Series Survey

- Repository: https://github.com/hiepnh137/multimodal-time-series-survey
- Relevant section: https://github.com/hiepnh137/multimodal-time-series-survey#retrieval-augmented-learning
- Its explicit **Retrieval-Augmented Learning** section contains Spectral Retrieval-Augmented Time-Series Forecasting, TRACE, TS-RAG and Time-VLM.
- Its organizing scope is multimodal time-series learning. It is useful for discovery, but does not provide the requested broad forecasting-only chronology plus SARAF taxonomy.
- The README contains 2026 literature; its precise latest update date was not independently established.

## Broad time-series catalogues

### TSFpaper

- Repository: https://github.com/ddz16/TSFpaper
- Broad forecasting list organized mainly by model family, with title/introduction, task definitions, news, contents and paper tables.
- Relevant papers are spread across MLP (RAFT), Generation (RATD), Plug and Play (RAF, TimeRAF, PFRP), LLM (TimeRAG, FinSeer), Foundation Models (TS-RAG), and Multi-Agent (Agentic-RAG).
- Its large README organization is a useful template. A dedicated retrieval view would bring together papers currently split across model families.
- The repository contains 2026 papers; no exact last-commit date is asserted here.

### Awesome Time Series Papers

- Repository: https://github.com/TSCenter/awesome-time-series-papers
- Organizes papers by task, year and venue, and includes RAFT, RATD, SARAF, QuiZSF and TS-RAG among a much wider set.
- README update notes include October 5, 2026 for NeurIPS 2026, and September 24 for KDD/ACL/VLDB additions.
- Useful for venue discovery and cross-checking; not a dedicated retrieval-method taxonomy.

## Adjacent thematic surveys

### MM4TSA

- Repository: https://github.com/AdityaLab/MM4TSA
- Survey: *How Can Time Series Analysis Benefit From Multiple Modalities?*
- Its Text to Time Series retrieval category includes TimeSeriesExam, CLaSP and TRACE.
- Cross-modal retrieval and time-series understanding are broader than retrieval-augmented forecasting. Entries require method-level screening before inclusion.

### Time-Series Reasoning Survey

- Repository: https://github.com/blacksnail789521/Time-Series-Reasoning-Survey
- Covers reasoning and agentic time-series analysis; relevant entries include RAF, TimeRAG, Agentic-RAG and FinSeer.
- Contains 2026 literature and accompanies a TMLR 2026 survey. It provides a complementary reasoning perspective rather than the requested retrieval-stage taxonomy.

### Awesome Agentic Time Series

- Repository: https://github.com/TROUBADOUR000/Awesome-Agentic-Time-Series
- Relevant entries include TS-RAG, FinSeer, Agentic-RAG, FLAIRR-TS, MemCast, TS-Memory and the earlier Factorize to Generalize title.
- The README includes June 2026 news and July entries. Its scope includes agents and memory mechanisms, including methods without inference-time retrieval.

### Awesome Multimodal LLMs for Time Series

- Repository: https://github.com/mllm-ts/Awesome-Multimodal-LLMs-Time-Series
- Accompanies the *Beyond Numbers* survey; includes agentic approaches such as Agentic-RAG.
- Relevant as an adjacent multimodal discovery source, with weaker overlap than the lists above.

### Awesome RAG

- Repository: https://github.com/liunian-Jay/Awesome-RAG
- A general RAG literature list that includes Cross-RAG.
- Broad discovery aid; it does not apply time-series forecasting-specific inclusion criteria.

## Recommended distinction

A useful new catalogue can distinguish itself through an explicit forecasting scope, two synchronized views, independently verified venues, version deduplication and a method-evidence trail. It should credit these related lists and avoid claims of being the first or exhaustive.
