# Ordering rules and notification-date sources

Updated 15 October 2026. These rules change presentation, not corpus membership or the master bibliography's first-public dates.

1. Years are descending. Accepted conference papers use the conference edition year, even when notification occurred in the previous calendar year. Journal records use an explicit publication/issue year when available; otherwise the retained bibliographic year is used. A preprint uses its retained public year, and unresolved publication timing remains separate.
2. Within a year, papers from the same venue are adjacent. Dated conference/workshop groups sort by notification date ascending. **These are venue-level ordering proxies, not individual-paper acceptance dates.** Paper-level acceptance dates remain unknown in the display-order metadata.
3. Main-track notification dates order combined venue groups with explicit track labels. AAAI AISI's own date was not verified. KDD 2026 is grouped together using its last scheduled research-track notification (16 May 2026); its other cycle notified on 23 November 2025, and the two catalogue papers' cycles are unknown. Neither date is silently assigned to those papers as their actual acceptance date.
4. Accepted conference/workshop venues with no verified notification date follow dated venues, then journals (alphabetical by journal), then records whose venue is unverified. **Preprints form the final group in each year.** Within a venue, titles are alphabetical because individual acceptance order is unverified. The order of undated groups does not claim acceptance chronology.
5. Main tracks, workshops and special tracks remain distinguishable in venue labels. arXiv links are retained for convenient reading of accepted papers; using an arXiv URL does not turn an accepted conference paper into a preprint.
6. The 127 records, 50 binary-mapped core methods, one hybrid and three inherited historical mappings are unchanged. Stable IDs, old scope decisions, source evidence and original first-public dates remain in the master data and crosswalk.
